Our Story

Rooted in the Wilderness

Empowering campers, mountaineers, and weekend adventurers across Myanmar with reliably structured, heavy-duty gear built to face the elements.

How Camp Sales Began

Founded by a small group of passionate local mountaineers, Camp Sales emerged from a simple realization: finding premium, water-resistant, and high-durability gear fine-tuned for Myanmar's unique tropical monsoon climate shouldn't be an adventure in itself.

What started as a modest inventory supply workshop in Yangon has scaled organically into a trusted nationwide direct-to-consumer distributor platform. We collaborate with international gear manufacturers to construct specialized solutions that endure rugged forest pathways, highlands, and coastal rain corridors.
